## Original Finding

English words, financial numbers, accessions, hashes, URLs and cursors. It was
used for successful tool data in the OAuth research transports but not the two
native API transports. Successful model text was also redacted per delta;
short deltas can reconstruct a known credential after emission. Managed
research stores text events before final-answer storage, so final-only
redaction does not protect every durable sink. These are synthetic/source-level
findings, not evidence of a production incident.
